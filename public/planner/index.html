hi, you.

This planner is for the girl with big plans and a very full head.

Made for real life

Size

A5, spiral bound

148 × 210 mm, with a 20 mm binding margin, so nothing important ever hides in the spiral.

Lines

7 mm writing lines

Generous, never cramped. Room for your real handwriting, on a bad day too.

Colour

Quiet inside, loud on the outside

Interior pages are black and white. Colour is saved for the covers, month openers and the pages that matter.

Months

Twelve watercolour openers

One painted scene and one line to start each month, in a ground colour of its own.

Weeks

Weekly spreads, honestly built

A single 'one thing' for the week, must-dos, and a place to write down how it actually went.

Rest

Pages for doing nothing

Mood tracker, habit wall, journal prompts and a whole page for doing nothing, on purpose.
